# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Its full text applies here; what follows is a summary and how to report a problem.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Disagree about ideas, code and designs, not about people.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully. Own your mistakes and learn from them.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome advances of any kind.
- Insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and wherever someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers by contacting the repository owner through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/khalaharvi). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers will review every report and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban someone, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
